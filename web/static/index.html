Overcoming the late-fusion information bottleneck via bidirectional cross-modal attention

Baseline Reference

M3DUSA Late Fusion

h_late = Linear(concat(h_text, h_graph))

Textual features (RoBERTa CLS) and social context embeddings (HGTConv mean-pooled) are extracted in absolute isolation and combined only via concatenation in the final classification projection. Misleading lexical claims cannot cross-examine the graph structure.

Novel Contribution · CMTF

Cross-Modal Transformer Fusion

Q_text · K_graph^T  ⟷  Q_graph · K_text^T

A 2-layer, 8-head bidirectional cross-attention module permits textual word tokens to attend across social graph nodes, while node embeddings attend back into linguistic cues. Deceptive vocabulary mimics are immediately flagged by discordant community topology.
