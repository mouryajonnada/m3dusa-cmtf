Methodology

Overcoming the late-fusion bottleneck via bidirectional cross-attention

Baseline

M3DUSA Late Fusion

h_late = Linear(concat(h_text, h_graph))

Text (RoBERTa CLS) and graph (GAT-pooled) representations are computed independently and concatenated at the final layer. Intermodal alignment is limited to parameter sharing in the classifier head — deceptive tokens cannot cross-reference social discourse markers.

Novel · CMTF

Cross-Modal Transformer Fusion

Q_text · K_graph^T  |  Q_graph · K_text^T

Two bidirectional cross-attention layers (8 heads, 256 dim) allow RoBERTa token vectors to attend over hashtag/evidence graph nodes and vice-versa. Deceptive claims that mimic authentic journalistic grammar are exposed by missing social consensus signals.
