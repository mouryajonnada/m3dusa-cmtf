Methodology: Overcoming the Late-Fusion Bottleneck

Baseline M3DUSA (Martirano et al., 2025)

h_late = Linear(concat(h_text, h_graph))

Text representation (RoBERTa CLS) and graph representation (GAT-pooled) are extracted independently and concatenated at the very final layer. The network cannot correlate individual deceptive words with specific social discourse markers.

Novel Cross-Modal Transformer Fusion (CMTF)

Q_text · K_graph^T  |  Q_graph · K_text^T

Stacks 2 bidirectional cross-attention layers with 8 heads. RoBERTa token vectors actively query hashtag and evidence nodes, unmasking deceptive claims that mimic authentic journalistic grammar by flagging missing social consensus.

Text Backbone: RoBERTa-base (768-dim, 12 layers)
Graph Backbone: 2-Layer HGTConv (256-dim, 4 heads)
Cross-Attention: 2 Layers, 8 Heads, 256-dim, Bidirectional
Dataset: PolitiFact (1,056 claims, 60/15/25 Stratified Split)
